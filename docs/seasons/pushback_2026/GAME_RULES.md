## Goals

### Long Goals (2)

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Capacity | 15 blocks |
| Location | One on each alliance side |
| Opening | Both ends open |

### Center Goals (2)

| Property | Value |
|----------|-------|
| Capacity | 7 blocks |
| Location | Center of field |
| Assignment | One Red, One Blue |

### Goal Mechanic: Push Back!

**The key mechanic:** Goals are open on both ends. When you push blocks into one side, excess blocks push out the other side!

```
Before:         Scoring:         After:
┌───────┐      ┌───────┐        ┌───────┐
│ ○ ○ ○ │  ○→  │○○ ○ ○ │   →○   │ ○ ○ ○ ○│
│(empty)│      │       │        │(full!) │
└───────┘      └───────┘        └───────┘
                                   ↑
                           Old blocks pushed out!
```

This creates dynamic gameplay - scoring for you might help your opponent!

## Scoring

### Points Summary

| Action | Points |
|--------|--------|
| Block in your goal | 3 points each |
| Goal control bonus | 10 points per goal |
| Single robot parked | 8 points |
| Both robots parked | 30 points (total) |

### Goal Control

A goal is "controlled" by the alliance with more blocks in it:
- More red blocks → Red controls
- More blue blocks → Blue controls
- Tie → No one controls

**Control bonus:** 10 points per goal you control at match end.

### Parking

| Condition | Points |
|-----------|--------|
| 1 robot parked | 8 points |
| 2 robots parked | 30 points |
| 0 robots parked | 0 points |

**Note:** Both robots parked gives a bonus (30 > 8 + 8 = 16).

## Scoring Examples

### Example 1: Basic Match

| Red | Blue |
|-----|------|
| 12 blocks in red goals | 10 blocks in blue goals |
| Control 2 goals | Control 2 goals |
| 1 robot parked | 0 robots parked |

**Red Score:**
- Blocks: 12 × 3 = 36
- Control: 2 × 10 = 20
- Parking: 8
- **Total: 64**

**Blue Score:**
- Blocks: 10 × 3 = 30
- Control: 2 × 10 = 20
- Parking: 0
- **Total: 50**

### Example 2: Parking Matters

Scenario: Tied on blocks and control, but...
- Red: 2 robots parked (30 pts)
- Blue: 1 robot parked (8 pts)

Red wins by 22 points on parking alone!

## Autonomous Bonus

There is no separate autonomous bonus. The value of autonomous is:
- Early scoring advantage
- Positioning for driver control
- Potential parking points

---

## Key Rules

### Robot Size

| Dimension | Limit |
|-----------|-------|
| Starting size | 18" × 18" × 18" |
| Expanded | No limit (but must start legal) |

### Possession

- Can possess multiple blocks
- No hoarding limits specified

### Defense

- Contact is allowed
- No pinning for more than 5 seconds
- No deliberate entanglement

### Autonomous Line

- Robots must start on their side
- Can cross to opponent side after autonomous starts

---

## Strategy Implications

### Offense vs Defense

**Offensive focus:**
- Score blocks in your goals
- Push out opponent blocks

**Defensive focus:**
- Block opponent scoring paths
- Push blocks back into opponent goals

### Parking Priority

At match end:
- 2 robots parked = 30 points
- That's equivalent to 10 blocks!
- Don't sacrifice parking for low-value scores

### Goal Control Math

| Blocks in Goal | Points (if controlled) |
|----------------|------------------------|
| 1 | 3 + 10 = 13 |
| 5 | 15 + 10 = 25 |
| 10 | 30 + 10 = 40 |

First block to control an empty goal = 13 points!
