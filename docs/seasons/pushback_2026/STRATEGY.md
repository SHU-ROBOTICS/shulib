## Game Analysis

### Key Observations

1. **Push Back mechanic is central**
   - Scoring pushes out opponent blocks
   - Goals are constantly in flux
   - Last-second scoring matters

2. **Parking is huge**
   - 30 points for both robots parked
   - Equivalent to 10 blocks
   - Can't be undone by opponent

3. **Goal control bonus**
   - 10 points per controlled goal
   - Having 1 more block than opponent = control
   - Worth fighting for at match end

4. **Block quantity matters less than timing**
   - Early blocks can be pushed out
   - Final state is what counts
   - Endgame positioning is critical

### Point Values Analysis

| Action | Points | Difficulty |
|--------|--------|------------|
| Score 1 block | 3 | Easy |
| Gain goal control | 10 | Medium |
| Park 2 robots | 30 | Requires coordination |
| Score 10 blocks | 30 | Takes significant time |

**Insight:** Parking 2 robots = scoring 10 blocks. Prioritize parking!

## Scoring Priorities

### Priority Order

1. **Secure parking** (30 pts)
   - Non-contestable once achieved
   - Huge point swing
   
2. **Control goals at end** (10 pts each)
   - Last blocks matter most
   - Focus on control, not quantity
   
3. **Deny opponent control**
   - Push their blocks out
   - Maintain presence at goals
   
4. **Score blocks** (3 pts each)
   - Important but volatile
   - Can be undone

### The "Last Block" Principle

Because of push-back, the LAST block scored in a goal often determines control:

```
Scenario: Goal has 5 red, 4 blue

Red scores → 6 red, 4 blue pushed? No, 6 red, 4 blue (Red control)
Blue scores → 5 red, 5 blue? Or 4 red, 5 blue? (Blue takes control!)
```

**Strategy:** Save some blocks for endgame "control battles."

## Autonomous Strategy

### Goals for Autonomous

1. **Score early** - Get blocks in before opponent
2. **Position well** - End near goals or blocks
3. **Don't overcommit** - 15 seconds is short

### Red Side Autonomous

```
Starting Position: Red starting zone

Option A: Rush Long Goal
- Drive straight to red long goal
- Score 2-3 blocks
- Back away

Option B: Center Goal Control
- Drive to red center goal
- Score 1-2 blocks for control
- Establishes presence

Option C: Block Collection
- Gather blocks toward our side
- Don't score yet
- Set up for driver control
```

### Blue Side Autonomous

Mirror of red side strategies.

### Skills Autonomous

For skills runs (1 minute):
- Maximize block scoring
- Visit all goals
- End with parking

## Driver Control Strategy

### Early Game (0:00 - 0:45)

**Focus: Block collection and positioning**

- Collect blocks efficiently
- Don't fight for goals yet
- Build up reserves
- Scout opponent strategy

### Mid Game (0:45 - 1:15)

**Focus: Scoring and control**

- Start filling goals
- Contest opponent goals
- Use push-back to your advantage
- Coordinate with alliance

### Late Game (1:15 - 1:45)

**Focus: Control and parking**

- Stop collecting, start finalizing
- Fight for goal control
- Push out opponent blocks
- Position for parking

### Final 15 Seconds

**Focus: Lock in points**

- One robot parks FIRST (non-negotiable)
- Second robot scores final blocks
- Second robot parks
- Don't get greedy - park!

## Endgame Strategy

### The 30-Point Park

Both robots parked = 30 points. This is HUGE.

**Endgame checklist:**
1. At 20 seconds: Both robots move toward parking
2. At 15 seconds: First robot enters parking zone
3. At 10 seconds: First robot secured, second approaches
4. At 5 seconds: Both robots in parking zone
5. At 0 seconds: Don't move!

### Goal Control Battles

If parking is secured, use remaining time:

```
Final 10 seconds:
- Partner 1: Parked, done
- Partner 2: Quick score in contested goal, then park
```

### Risk Assessment

| Risk Level | Action |
|------------|--------|
| Low | Both park early, accept current score |
| Medium | One parks, one scores, then parks |
| High | Both score until last moment, rush to park |

**Recommendation:** Low-medium risk. Parking is worth too much.

## Alliance Coordination

### Role Division

**Robot A: Scorer**
- Focus on collecting and scoring
- Contest goals
- Higher mobility

**Robot B: Defender/Parker**
- Block opponent paths
- Secure parking early
- Support scorer

### Communication

Pre-match agreement:
- "I'll go left, you go right"
- "I'll park first at 15 seconds"
- "Call out when you're parking"

### Scenarios

**Winning:** Protect lead, park early, don't risk points

**Tied:** Balance scoring and parking, coordinate final push

**Losing:** Must score aggressively, park at last possible moment

## Counter-Strategies

### Against Aggressive Scorers

- Let them fill goals, then push blocks back
- Focus on control, not quantity
- Park while they're distracted

### Against Defensive Robots

- Work around them
- Score in undefended goals
- Don't waste time fighting

### Against Fast Robots

- Control key areas early
- Use alliance to block paths
- Accept some scoring, secure parking

### Against Push-Back Specialists

- Score in clusters to make push-back hard
- Contest both ends of goals
- Don't leave goals unattended
