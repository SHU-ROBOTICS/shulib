### 🔴 OPEN DEFECT — the hard floor can silently not fire

Found by the guarantee probe. When the **end action** ticks a mechanism operation through
`guard.waitFor`, `MotionScheduler::waitUntil` checks the predicate **before** `pace()` — so the
guard's own predicate observes the floor and returns **without `pace()` ever running `fireFloor()`**.

Measured:

```
guard.waitFor returned control at LEDGER T+14.5100s (hard floor T+14.50)
intake |V| AT that instant (past the floor)          = 9.000 V
intake |V| after run() returned                      = 0.000 V
report.floorFired = 0
fireFloor()'s "all devices safed unconditionally" Warn logged: NO
```

The devices **are** safed when `run()` returns, so this is not a runaway. But the "unconditional"
floor did not fire, `report.floorFired` is **false**, and **no log line records it** — so a run where
the floor mattered looks identical to one where it never came up. **Fix it, and add the case to
`verify-f2.sh`'s mutation set.**

### 🟡 HONESTY GAP — the zero-travel claim is plant-shaped

The latch probe is explicit: the `0.0000000000 in` depends jointly on check-then-step ordering **and
`kA = 0`** in the A2 plant. On a robot with mass, a cut at the deadline leaves brake/coast distance
this plant cannot produce. **The honest on-robot claim is "no new commanded motion", not "zero
travel"** — the guide currently implies the stronger one. Fix the wording.

And a second, sharper one for Phase R: **on a real brain, time passes whether or not `pace()` is
called.** Both the latch and the floor can only fire when the caller re-enters shulib. That belongs
in the honesty section beside "nothing preempts pure user code".

## What F2 built, and the edges it refuses to overstate

`sequence/run_guard.hpp` — an `ITickPacer` decorator, **inert until `run()`**, that owns the run-scoped
deadline and fires a **caller-supplied** end action. D-8 and the guaranteed end-of-run action are one
primitive with two policies, which is what E1 said when it re-homed the debt.

**It cannot:** claim real-brain timing margins (loop rate and PROS latency are invented until R4);
preempt pure user code (no background tasks exist); cut the frozen F10/F6 waits, which pay their full
remainder — **a limit that is tested rather than hidden**; or end an unconditional retry loop.

**It does not know your strategy.** No field coordinate, no park pose, no default lead time, no
default match length. `endInMidfield` stays F4's, with the students' numbers.
