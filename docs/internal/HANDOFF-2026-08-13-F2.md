## What F2 built, and the edges it refuses to overstate

`sequence/run_guard.hpp` — an `ITickPacer` decorator, **inert until `run()`**, that owns the run-scoped
deadline and fires a **caller-supplied** end action. D-8 and the guaranteed end-of-run action are one
primitive with two policies, which is what E1 said when it re-homed the debt.

**It cannot:** claim real-brain timing margins (loop rate and PROS latency are invented until R4);
preempt pure user code (no background tasks exist); cut the frozen F10/F6 waits, which pay their full
remainder — **a limit that is tested rather than hidden**; or end an unconditional retry loop.

**It does not know your strategy.** No field coordinate, no park pose, no default lead time, no
default match length. `endInMidfield` stays F4's, with the students' numbers.

## Two Rule 4 fixes landed in earlier chunks' code

1. **F1** — an operation destroyed mid-flight left its mechanism **claimed forever and energized** (no
   destructor; rule-of-three violation). Now cancel-on-destruction plus deleted copy/move.
2. **C2** — both blocking waits could strand an armed motion on unwind, the root cause of the
   **11.4 V-under-Coast** state measured during the F2 probe campaign. Fixed at the loop owner with
   `WaitUnwindGuard`, which also closes the `Chassis::waitUntil` gap without touching frozen F6.
   **This is the change the `rule4-unwind` probe must vet.**

Earlier in the same session, two more: the **version pins were vacuous** (`kApiMinor >= 0` can never
fail, and a one-character check could not see `kApiVersionString` drifting), and **both freeze pins
were blind to a frozen member GAINING `noexcept`** — measured, with the failure landing as a stale-docs
message and a SIGABRT rather than a named freeze violation.
